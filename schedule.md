# Tutorial Schedule: From Quantum Modeling to Execution

The tutorial comprises two tightly connected sessions that guide participants from the conceptual foundations of quantum application modeling to the hands-on execution of a complete quantum workflow using a low-code environment. Participants will progress from high-level quantum abstractions to an end-to-end executable quantum algorithm pipeline.

---

## Tutorial Structure

### Session 1: Quantum Modeling Foundations and Abstractions
* **Introduction:** Overview of quantum computing concepts relevant for software engineering, including current challenges in circuit-level development and the need for higher-level abstractions.
* **Visual Quantum Modeling:** Introduction to low-code, model-driven representations of quantum algorithms using domain-specific visual building blocks.
* **Quantum Execution Architecture:** Conceptual overview of compilation pipelines and execution workflows across heterogeneous quantum backends.

---

### Session 2: Quantum Algorithm Modeling and Execution Pipeline
* **Hands-On Lab:** Step-by-step construction of a quantum algorithm (Grover’s Search) using a visual modeling environment, followed by automatic compilation to OpenQASM3 and execution via a middleware orchestration layer.
* **Evaluation & Feedback:** Interactive Q&A session and feedback collection to evaluate usability, abstraction effectiveness, and workflow integration.
