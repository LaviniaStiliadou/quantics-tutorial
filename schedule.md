# Tutorial Schedule & Structure

The tutorial consists of two sessions combining focused presentations with hands-on activities. It is designed for participants with prior knowledge of quantum computing who are interested in model-driven engineering, low-code development, and platform-independent quantum application design and execution.

---


### Session 1: Low-Code Design & Quantum Application Execution

* **Motivation & Context:** Challenges in quantum software engineering, including portability, maintainability, and abstraction across heterogeneous quantum ecosystems.
* **Low-Code Modeling:** Model-driven & low-code approaches for separating quantum application design from hardware-specific implementation details using visual modeling techniques.
* **Execution Orchestration Middleware:** Unified deployment and execution of quantum applications across multiple providers through intermediate representations and workflow orchestration.
* **Hands-On Lab:** Collaborative modeling of Grover's search algorithm, generation of OpenQASM 3 artifacts and workflows, followed by deployment and execution using Qunicorn & Camunda.

### Session 2: Automated Model Generation and Toolchain Feedback

* **Hands-On Lab:** Exploring algorithm selection mechanisms and automated generation of executable quantum low-code models from higher-level specifications.
* **Discussion & Feedback:** Interactive discussion on usability, modeling abstractions, execution workflows, and future extensions of the low-code toolchain.
